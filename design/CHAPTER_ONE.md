# Chapter 1 Spec — Hollow Township

> Status: FROZEN. Beat structure, moral choice weight system, and critical design rules.

---

## Chapter 1 Purpose

Chapter 1 is the complete emotional promise delivered in preview form.

The player must experience the full RELIEF → UNEASE → COMPULSION loop within this chapter. They must leave with earned dread, not manufactured atmosphere.

**Target playtime**: 3–4 hours.
(Research basis: horror session sweet spot is 2–4 hours peak engagement; engagement cliff appears after hour 4 without pacing variety; Early Access Chapter 1 framing requires enough content to justify purchase, not a demo length of 18–65 min.)

---

## Moral Choice Weight System

Three tiers, all active from minute 1.

### Micro (every 10–15 min)
- Feels mechanical, invisible as moral choice
- Examples: where to place a building, which NPC gets a job, who eats first
- All connected to named NPCs
- Cost surfaces in the next night cycle or next day cycle
- Player recognizes the moral weight only in retrospect

### Mid (every 30–45 min)
- Slightly uncomfortable — player senses but cannot name the weight
- Examples: faction negotiation, resource prioritization under scarcity, who to assign to a dangerous role
- Cost surfaces within the same day or same night as the choice
- Player connects cause and effect, but with a delay

### Macro (once per chapter beat)
- Explicitly moral — the player knows what they're choosing
- Examples: sacrifice one to save many, betray a faction to survive a night, make a promise you cannot keep
- Immediate impact + delayed second-order effect (the second-order effect surfaces later in the chapter)
- Player already implicated by accumulated micro/mid choices — macro lands heavy because of this buildup

**Critical rule**: The player should not always know they are making a moral choice. Logistics *are* moral choices. The reveal is the game.

## Chapter 1 Beat Structure

```
Hour 0:00–0:30   DAY 1
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Onboarding through action, not tutorial.
3–4 micro choices embedded in placement and assignment.
None labeled as moral. All connected to named NPCs.
Player meets 4–6 named NPCs with distinct voices.
Tone: purposeful tension, false sense of control.

Hour 0:30–1:00   NIGHT 1
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2 micro choices from Day 1 surface as night consequences.
First "Watched" feeling lands before player can articulate why.
Horror is low-intensity but traceable — player senses the connection.
Tone: unease, not panic. The wrongness is quiet.

Hour 1:00–1:45   DAY 2
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Player now plays with awareness — they know choices matter.
3–4 more micro choices, felt more acutely this time.
One mid-weight choice emerges from a faction responding to Day 1.
Named NPCs show behavioral change based on Night 1 outcome.
Tone: heightened attention, compulsion activating.

Hour 1:45–2:30   NIGHT 2
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Consequences from Day 1 AND Day 2 surface together.
Player sees the web for the first time.
Compulsion spikes — player wants to go back and choose differently.
At least one named NPC is in danger traced to player's Day 1 micro choice.
Tone: the game looking back at you.

Hour 2:30–3:30   DAY 3 + NIGHT 3
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
First macro choice.
Player is already implicated — micro and mid choices have accumulated.
Macro lands heavy because it asks the player to choose who they are,
  after demonstrating who they already have been.
Night 3 delivers chapter climax: horror shaped by all prior choices.
Chapter closes on an unresolved win.
Tone: compulsion, not satisfaction.
Player cannot immediately articulate all the choices that got them here.
```

---

## Chapter End State

The chapter ends when:
- The first macro choice has been made and its immediate consequences have landed
- At least one named NPC's arc has advanced (better or worse, not neutral)
- The RELIEF → UNEASE → COMPULSION loop has completed twice (Night 1 and Night 2/3)
- The player feels implicated, not victimized

**Chapter end emotion target**: *"I need to play Chapter 2 to find out if I can fix this."*
Not: *"I finished the chapter."*

---

## Named NPC Requirements for Chapter 1

- Minimum 4 named NPCs introduced in Hour 1
- Each NPC must have: a name, a visible want, and a visible fear
- At least 2 NPCs must show behavioral change by Night 2 based on player micro choices
- At least 1 NPC must be in a worse position at chapter end than at chapter start, traceable to player action

---

## What Chapter 1 Must Not Do

- Do not resolve the horror — the player should leave with open dread
- Do not reward "good" play with a clean ending — every ending is compromised
- Do not introduce macro choices before Hour 2:30 — the accumulation is required for macro weight
- Do not let night horror be random — every horror event must trace to a day choice
