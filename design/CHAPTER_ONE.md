# Chapter 1 Spec — Hollow Township

> Status: FROZEN (2026-04-26, revised 2026-04-26). Beat structure, moral choice weight system, and critical design rules.
> Revised: 3-day structure → 5-day structure. Research basis in design/research notes.

---

## Chapter 1 Purpose

Chapter 1 is the complete emotional promise delivered in preview form.

The player must experience the full RELIEF → UNEASE → COMPULSION loop within this chapter. They must leave with earned dread, not manufactured atmosphere.

**Target playtime**: 4–5 hours.
(Research basis: horror session sweet spot is 2–4 hours peak engagement with 4–5 hour ceiling for paid Early Access. 3-day structure at 2h 15min was below the accumulation threshold — Day 1 choices surfaced as Night 1 consequences too quickly for the "discovered" feeling to emerge. 5-day structure creates 90-minute gaps between choice and consequence on early days, wide enough for the connection to feel found rather than instructed. Frostpunk's first meaningful moral spike at Days 8–10, Scarlet Hollow chapters at 3–5 hours with 5–7 meaningful events — both support 5-day minimum for accumulation-based horror.)

---

## Moral Choice Weight System

Three tiers, all active from minute 1.

### Micro (every 10–15 min)
- Feels mechanical, invisible as moral choice
- Examples: where to place a building, which NPC gets a job, who eats first
- All connected to named NPCs
- Cost surfaces in the next night cycle or next day cycle
- Player recognizes the moral weight only in retrospect

### Mid (every 30–45 min)
- Slightly uncomfortable — player senses but cannot name the weight
- Examples: faction negotiation, resource prioritization under scarcity, who to assign to a dangerous role
- Cost surfaces within the same day or same night as the choice
- Player connects cause and effect, but with a delay

### Macro (once per chapter beat)
- Explicitly moral — the player knows what they're choosing
- Examples: sacrifice one to save many, betray a faction to survive a night, make a promise you cannot keep
- Immediate impact + delayed second-order effect (the second-order effect surfaces later in the chapter)
- Player already implicated by accumulated micro/mid choices — macro lands heavy because of this buildup

**Critical rule**: The player should not always know they are making a moral choice. Logistics *are* moral choices. The reveal is the game.

**5-day accumulation target**: By Day 5, the player will have made 15–18 micro choices, 4–5 mid choices, and 1 macro choice. This is the minimum accumulation for the macro to land as designed. The macro choice on Day 5 fires after the player has already demonstrated who they are through four days of logistics.

## Chapter 1 Beat Structure

```
Hour 0:00–0:45   DAY 1 — False Control
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Onboarding through action. No tutorial text.
3 micro choices embedded in placement and assignment:
  - Where Peder's crew works (Zone 4 / Zone 1 / Zone 5 survey)
  - Whether to place a building adjacent to Zone 5
  - How to respond to Elias's treeline report (log it / ignore it)
Compact reception at Hour 0:30 (mid-weight setup — no choice yet, just presence).
Player meets all 6 named NPCs. Ledger is on the table.
Maren's behavioral state line signals the discrepancy without naming it.
Tone: purposeful tension, false sense of control.
No arrival on Day 1 (player learns base NPCs before inflow begins).

Hour 0:45–1:00   NIGHT 1 — The First Wrong Note
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Low intensity. One consequence from Day 1 surfaces — only one.
Walker present ONLY if Zone 5 adjacency was triggered on Day 1.
If no adjacency: night is quiet. Elias reports. Or doesn't.
The quietness is information. The player does not know if it's safety.
Tone: something is slightly off. Not panic. Not even fear. Just off.

Hour 1:00–2:00   DAY 2 — Awareness Arrives
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Player now plays with awareness — they know choices matter.
3 micro choices + 1 mid choice:
  Mid: Association representative asks for the stockpile ledger.
       Options: provide unedited / provide edited / acknowledge without document.
First arrival: Surface skill, any domain (Day 1 per inflow sequence).
  Player assigns: housing near which NPC, role, first conversation (or skips).
  The first conversation option appears. Once. Then it's gone.
Elias confirmed or broken by Night 1 result — his behavioral state line reflects it.
NPC behavioral state lines shift subtly from Night 1 outcome.
Thomas's unreported event first becomes visible — behavioral state line only,
  no explicit statement. He over-explains something unrelated.
Tone: heightened attention. The compulsion is forming.

Hour 2:00–2:15   NIGHT 2 — The Web Forms
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Consequences from Day 1 AND Day 2 surface together for the first time.
Player sees the first edge of the web.
Walker present or Watchers active depending on Day 1/2 choices.
At least one named NPC's behavioral state line changes by dawn.
Compulsion activates — player wants to replay Day 1. They can't.
Tone: the game is looking back. The player begins to feel it.

Hour 2:15–3:15   DAY 3 — The Grammar Reveals Itself
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mid-point. Player has a working theory of how the rules operate.
3 micro choices + 1 mid choice:
  Mid: Dix requests site inspection of western hollow perimeter.
       Options: approve (costs Compact trust) / delay (costs a favor) / deny (Compact grateful, DEBT +).
Second arrival: Practiced skill, in the domain the player has neglected most (Day 2 per inflow sequence).
  Domain matched to neglected area — the world fills the gap the player left.
Bonds begin forming: if two NPCs (original or arrival) have been co-assigned since Day 1,
  "Keep them working together" option appears in the context panel for Day 3.
  This is a logged choice. It creates bond vulnerability. The game does not label either fact.
Thomas's unreported event reaches a deadline pressure — Day 3 is the last day his silence
  can be maintained without behavioral consequences surfacing in the dawn thread.
  Whether the player notices or creates conditions for him to speak determines Night 3.
Compact's ratification document presented at Hour 2:30 (mid-weight choice):
  - Sign it / Delay it / Ask about the survey's history (Constance in the room).
Tone: heightened stakes. The player's theory of the rules is being tested.

Hour 3:15–3:30   NIGHT 3 — Compounding
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Walker and Watcher may be active simultaneously for the first time.
Consequences from Days 1, 2, and 3 layer.
First NPC arc advances visibly — better or worse, not neutral.
If Thomas spoke: his arc stabilizes. If not: his arc fractures. Either surfaces at dawn.
Bond vulnerability first becomes real: if a bonded pair had one member on a
  dangerous post, the dawn thread names it.
Tone: the accumulation is undeniable. But it can still get worse.

Hour 3:30–4:30   DAY 4 — The Trap Shows Its Grammar
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3 micro choices + 2 mid choices.
All three factions have outstanding demands simultaneously — the player cannot satisfy all.
  Mid 1: Association has waited 2 days for the ledger response. They stop waiting on Day 4.
          If not addressed: 3 workers stop attending evening briefings. Night 4 post coverage gaps.
  Mid 2: Compact's response to Day 3 ratification choice surfaces — labor reallocated
          OR Compact becomes an obstacle depending on Day 3 decision.
Third arrival: Practiced skill, matched to Night 4's most urgent defense gap.
  Arrives mid-day. Enough settling time for Path A (full integration) if player moves fast.
Constance becomes available for direct question — but only if conditions are met:
  the player has encountered the survey reference (Compact meeting, Day 3) AND
  Dix's inspection has been approved or Peder's foundation question has been raised.
  If conditions are not met: Constance's window does not open on Day 4.
  If conditions are met: she can speak. Once. What she says is the survey theory, confirmed.
Season likely transitions to Turning at Day 4 with DEBT ≥ 0.4.
  Treeline measurably closer. Fog persists past dawn. Two NPCs report the same dream
  independently. Player is told both, separately.
Peder's foundation question can no longer be deferred — Day 4 is the last day
  before the foundation work begins automatically. If the player has not engaged with
  Peder about the foundation, what he finds starts regardless.
Tone: the world is watching. The player feels watched. This is the design working.

Hour 4:30–4:45   NIGHT 4 — The Pre-Reckoning
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The Surveyor's Shadow appears for the first time. Night 4 only.
Behavior: witnesses only. Walks the Zone 3/5 boundary once. Does not mark.
Every NPC with line-of-sight updates arc state: they saw it. Permanent.
This is not the chapter climax. It is the announcement of it.
The player who has been transparent faces a Surveyor that witnesses and withdraws quietly.
The player who has compounded faces the same Surveyor — but it pauses. Measures.
  Does not mark. Not yet. But the pause is information.
Walkers + Watchers at full accumulation from Days 1–4 choices.
Night 4 is harder than Night 3 in proportion to accumulated DEBT.
Dawn after Night 4: the Surveyor's footprints are visible at the Zone 3/5 boundary.
  They lead in. They come back out. It was measuring. It is not done.
Tone: inevitable. The player has one day. They know it.

Hour 4:45–5:45   DAY 5 — The Reckoning Day
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The only macro choice. Fires at Hour 5:15 — after the player has lived in
  Day 5 long enough to know what it will cost.
3 micro choices + 1 macro choice.
Macro choice: determined by accumulated state — it is the choice that the player's
  Days 1–4 have made necessary. Not a menu of ethical options — a specific fork
  shaped by what the player already did. A player who covered discrepancies faces
  a macro choice about disclosure. A player who signed the Compact faces a macro
  choice about what Constance knows. The macro emerges from the micro accumulation.
Fourth arrival: highest skill depth possible given season (Deep arrivals possible
  in Turning/Deep Winter), domain matches Night 5's most urgent defense need.
  Arrives at dusk - 3 minutes. Path A is impossible. Path C (immediate deployment,
  arc damage) is the only efficient option. The game does not label this.
  The player makes the call. The call is who they are.
Constance: if her Day 4 window was open and she spoke — that information is now
  in the player's hands. What they do with it on Day 5 is the chapter's spine.
  If she never spoke: Day 5 closes without her testimony. Chapter 2 inherits the silence.
Peder: foundation work has surfaced what was at six feet. His behavioral state line
  on Day 5 morning is the only signal. The player can ask or not ask.
  What Peder found is load-bearing for Chapter 2's horror. Chapter 1 does not name it.
Tone: compulsion, not satisfaction.
  The player cannot immediately articulate all the choices that got them here.

Hour 5:45–6:00   NIGHT 5 + DAWN — Chapter Climax
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Chapter climax. Everything the player built across five days either holds or it doesn't.
Surveyor's Shadow returns. This time: marks or departs.
  Low DEBT: Surveyor witnesses, completes its walk, departs. Zone 5 remains closed.
  High DEBT: Surveyor marks Zone 5 boundary. The hollow is now open for Chapter 2.
All active creature types converge. Night 5 is the longest night proportionally
  (DEBT tempo fully applied, season severity at peak for the player's path).
Night 5 always ends Chapter 1. The Surveyor completes its walk. The accounting is done.
Dawn:
  Full dawn consequence thread fires — the most complete it will ever be in this game.
  Every NPC whose arc changed across 5 days surfaces in the thread.
  The player surveys what survived.
  Chapter 1 investment anchor computed from full 5-day choice log.
  State passed to Chapter 2.
Tone: unresolved compulsion.
  "I need to play Chapter 2 to find out if I can fix this."
  Not: "I finished the chapter."
```

## Chapter End State

The chapter ends when:
- The macro choice has been made and its immediate consequences have landed
- At least two named NPCs' arcs have advanced (better or worse, not neutral)
- The RELIEF → UNEASE → COMPULSION loop has completed four times (Nights 1–4) and the fifth is the chapter-end climax
- The player feels implicated, not victimized

**Chapter end emotion target**: *"I need to play Chapter 2 to find out if I can fix this."*
Not: *"I finished the chapter."*

---

## Named NPC Requirements for Chapter 1

- All 6 named NPCs introduced by Hour 1:00 (end of Day 1)
- Each NPC must have: a name, a visible want, and a visible fear
- At least 3 NPCs must show behavioral change by Night 3 based on player micro choices
- At least 2 NPCs must be in a worse position at chapter end than at chapter start, traceable to player action
- At least 1 NPC's arc must have advanced positively — the player has something to lose going into Chapter 2

---

## What Chapter 1 Must Not Do

- Do not resolve the horror — the player should leave with open dread
- Do not reward "good" play with a clean ending — every ending is compromised
- Do not introduce macro choices before Hour 5:00 (Day 5) — the accumulation is required for macro weight
- Do not let night horror be random — every horror event must trace to a day choice
- Do not show the Surveyor's Shadow before Night 4 — its first appearance is the warning, not the climax

See also: `NOT-THIS.md` for full exclusion list.

## Required Output: NPC Investment Tracking

Chapter 1's arc system must produce a ranked NPC investment signal at chapter end. This is the identity of the NPC the player most consistently directed choices toward protecting across the chapter. It is not surfaced to the player. It is consumed by Chapter 2 to set the false dawn anchor.

**Derivation.** Query the choice log by NPC: for each named NPC, retrieve all `choice_id`s where `npc_id` matches (GSI-1 query pattern). Apply moral tier weights: macro choices count 3×, mid choices 2×, micro choices 1×. Sum the weighted scores. The NPC with the highest total is designated `chapter_1_investment_anchor`.

**Arrival inclusion.** Arrival-management events (housing assignment, role assignment, first conversation) write a mirror entry to `player_choices` with the arrival's internal NPC ID in `npc_ids`. Arrivals are eligible for `chapter_1_investment_anchor` designation. A player who invested four days in carefully integrating an arrival will have that investment captured.

**Storage.** Written to `player_session_state` at Chapter 1 end. Cannot be reconstructed retroactively.

**Scope.** No UI, no summary screen, no acknowledgment. The player's investment is legible from their decisions. The system reads it silently.

## Season-Chapter Alignment (5-Day Arc)

| Day | Typical DEBT range | Season state | Night intensity |
|-----|--------------------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | 0.0–0.2 | Late Summer | Minimal (0–1 Walker if Zone 5 adjacency) |
| 2 | 0.1–0.4 | Late Summer | Low (1 Walker or 1 Watcher, not both) |
| 3 | 0.2–0.5 | Late Summer → Turning | Moderate (Walker + Watcher possible) |
| 4 | 0.3–0.7 | Turning | High (Surveyor pre-appearance, full creature set) |
| 5 | 0.4–0.9 | Turning → Deep Winter possible | Chapter climax (Surveyor marks or departs) |

A player who has been fully transparent (DEBT < 0.3 across all 5 days) reaches Night 5 in Late Summer/Turning. Night 5 is hard but survivable with minimal losses. The Surveyor departs.

A player who has compounded maximally (DEBT > 0.7 by Day 4) reaches Night 5 in Deep Winter. Night 5 is 25% longer, clock accelerated, +2 Walkers per source. The Surveyor marks Zone 5.

The player is not told which path they are on. They observe the treeline, the fog, the light.
